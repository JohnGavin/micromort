Data summary

- **26 countries** — top 20 by population plus
  [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/about/members-and-partners/) member states
- **14 causes of death** — 7
  [non-communicable](https://www.who.int/health-topics/noncommunicable-diseases) +
  7 [infectious](https://www.who.int/health-topics/infectious-diseases)
- **Metric:** age-standardised death rate per 100,000 population
- **Source:** [IHME Global Burden of
  Disease](https://www.healthdata.org/research-analysis/gbd) 2019, via
  [OWID](https://ourworldindata.org/causes-of-death) catalog (snapshot
  `2024-05-20`)
- Percentage shares are relative to these 14 tracked causes only —
  **not** 100% of all-cause mortality

Exclusions

- **Ireland** — listed in the download script's country filter (`IRL`)
  but absent from the bundled OWID snapshot. Requires [IHME GBD Results
  Tool](https://www.healthdata.org/data-tools-practices/interactive-visuals/gbd-results)
  direct download — see
  [\#94](https://github.com/JohnGavin/micromort/issues/94)
- **Injuries** (road accidents, self-harm, violence, falls, drowning) —
  a major cause-of-death category, especially in younger age groups. Not
  yet included — see
  [\#91](https://github.com/JohnGavin/micromort/issues/91)
- **Maternal and neonatal deaths** — account for ~10% of deaths in
  low-income countries. Not yet included
- **Other NCDs** (neurological, mental, musculoskeletal) — not in the 7
  tracked NCD categories

**Data staleness warning**  
[GBD](https://www.healthdata.org/research-analysis/gbd) data year:
**2019** — the most recent year in the bundled snapshot.  
OWID catalog date: **2024-05-20** — this URL now returns HTTP 404. OWID
restricted GBD redistribution due to IHME licensing changes.  
[GBD
2023](https://ghdx.healthdata.org/record/ihme-data/gbd-2023-cause-specific-mortality-1990-2023)
is available from IHME (free account required) — see
[\#90](https://github.com/JohnGavin/micromort/issues/90).

Glossary

- [GBD](https://www.healthdata.org/research-analysis/gbd) — Global
  Burden of Disease study (IHME)
- [IHME](https://www.healthdata.org/) — Institute for Health Metrics and
  Evaluation
- [OWID](https://ourworldindata.org/) — Our World in Data
- [NCD](https://www.who.int/health-topics/noncommunicable-diseases) —
  Non-communicable diseases

  Cardiovascular disease

  — Disability-adjusted life year
